# Geographic Opportunity

## State-Level Prevalence Variation (FACT)
ICMR-INDIAB-derived analysis (Scientific Reports, 2025) identifies **Kerala, Jammu & Kashmir, Delhi, Haryana, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh** as having over half of their adult population at high risk of Type 2 diabetes — meaning the highest-prevalence states span both high-income (Kerala, Delhi) and mid-income (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana) profiles, not just wealthy metros.

## Segmentation Framework
| Geography Tier | Characteristics | Insulin Strategy Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Metro (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) | High diagnosis rates, strong specialist access, higher insurance penetration | Premium analogue + device-led strategy |
| Tier 1 cities (state capitals, large industrial cities) | Growing diagnosis, general-physician-led care, moderate infrastructure | Segmented portfolio: analogue + premix human insulin |
| Tier 2 towns | Large population base, RSSDI/regional diabetologist networks, cost-sensitive | Human insulin/premix-led, with analogue upsell via patient support |
| Tier 3+ / rural | Highest unmet need, weakest cold chain and last-mile distribution, PMBJP/government-channel dependent | Access-led: government partnership, PMBJP-aligned SKUs, cold-chain investment |

## Priority Geography Recommendation (STRATEGIC JUDGMENT)
Given the mandate to expand access while protecting economics, the highest-value near-term geographic focus is **Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities in high-prevalence, mid-income states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, and similar)** — large enough patient pools to justify commercial investment, less price-suppressed than Tier 3+/rural, and under-served relative to metro markets that are already well-penetrated by both Novo Nordisk and competitors.

## Rural/Tier 3+ as a Phase 2 Priority
Rural and Tier 3+ geographies carry the largest *unmet need* but require supply-chain investment (cold chain, distribution) before commercial investment in demand generation pays off — sequencing this as a Year 2–3 priority avoids stranding investment in un-servable geographies (see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/implementation-roadmap.md`).
