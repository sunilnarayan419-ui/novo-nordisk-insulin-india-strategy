# KPI Framework

## Market Access / Volume KPIs
- Number of insulin-treated patients served (by channel, by tier) — target trajectory per `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/outputs/scenario_summary.csv`
- Number of PMBJP/Jan Aushadhi outlets reliably stocked with insulin (cold-chain-verified)
- Number of patients enrolled in the Type 1 diabetes patient-support program
- Number of institutional volume contracts signed (private hospital networks, PM-JAY–empanelled hospitals)

## Commercial / Financial KPIs
- Revenue and gross margin by channel/tier, tracked against Base/Upside/Downside model bands
- Blended gross margin % (tracked for early warning of unplanned margin compression — see `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/sensitivity-analysis/sensitivity-summary.md`)
- Novo Nordisk share of SAM by channel (see `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/TAM-SAM-SOM.md`)

## Operational KPIs
- Stock-out incidence rate (by channel, by geography)
- Cold-chain breach/wastage rate (by geography, tracked as investment in `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md` mitigation scales)
- Order-to-delivery lead time (institutional and retail channels)

## Clinical/Access Outcome KPIs (Proxy Measures, Given Data Limitations)
- Physician-reported reduction in insulin-initiation delay (survey-based, given lack of a national registry)
- Patient-support program retention/persistence rate (proxy for real-world adherence improvement)
- Insurer/TPA pilot: claims-cost trend for enrolled vs. non-enrolled comparable cohorts (evidence for the reimbursement-advocacy case)

## Review Cadence
Monthly operational KPI review (cross-functional working group); quarterly commercial/financial KPI review (executive steering committee); annual full-model recalibration against updated public data (new ICMR-INDIAB release, new NPPA notifications) per `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/README.md`.
