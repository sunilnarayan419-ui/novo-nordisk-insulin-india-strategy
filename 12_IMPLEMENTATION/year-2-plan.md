# Year 2 Plan

## Focus: Scale What Worked, Begin Ecosystem Build
1. **Geographic expansion**: extend segmented portfolio/pricing rollout to additional Tier 1/2 priority states and begin phased Tier 3+/rural entry in geographies where Year 1 cold-chain investment has been completed.
2. **Digital adherence tool launch**: pilot a simplified digital companion (dose reminders, basic glucose-log integration) bundled with mainstream-tier and premium-tier products in 2–3 metro/Tier 1 markets.
3. **Institutional partnership scale-up**: convert Year 1 pilot insurer/TPA partnership into a scaled program if pilot KPIs (claims-cost avoidance evidence) are positive; expand volume-contract private hospital network.
4. **Provider training scale-up**: expand simplified-initiation-protocol training from pilot Tier 2 cities to a broader general-physician network, potentially via a train-the-trainer / diabetes educator certification model.
5. **PMBJP partnership scale-up**: expand insulin-specific cold-chain distribution to additional Jan Aushadhi Kendras beyond the Year 1 pilot geography.

## Review Gate
End-of-Year-2 review against Base Case model milestones (see `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/README.md`); if performance is tracking toward the Upside Case, accelerate ecosystem investment per `year-3-5-plan.md`; if tracking toward Downside Case, prioritize margin-protection actions (see `13_RISK_ANALYSIS/mitigation-plan.md`) before further access-investment scale-up.
