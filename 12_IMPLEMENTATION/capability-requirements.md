# Capability Requirements

## New/Strengthened Capabilities Needed
1. **Channel pricing governance** — internal systems and controls to manage differentiated pricing across channels without enabling arbitrage/leakage (a capability gap for organizations used to single-price-list commercial models).
2. **Government/institutional partnership management** — dedicated market-access function capable of sustained engagement with NHA, state health departments, PMBJP/Department of Pharmaceuticals, and insurers — distinct from traditional physician-detailing sales capability.
3. **Digital product capability** — either in-house or partnered capability to build/maintain a patient-facing digital adherence tool, including data-privacy-compliant design for health information in India.
4. **Health economics/real-world-evidence capability** — to eventually close the evidentiary gap identified in `08_HEALTH_ECONOMICS/clinical-outcome-value.md` (no India-specific public cost-effectiveness data currently exists) and support future payer/policy engagement with locally generated evidence.
5. **Supply-chain/cold-chain program management** — capability to manage geography-prioritized infrastructure investment and partnership execution (distinct from core manufacturing/logistics operations).
6. **Patient-support program operations** — capability to run co-pay assistance and enrollment programs at scale, including NGO partnership management for the Type 1 diabetes program.

## Build vs. Partner Recommendation
For capabilities 3 (digital) and 6 (patient support), a **partner-first approach** (established digital health platforms, patient-assistance NGOs) is recommended over building fully in-house, given faster time-to-value and lower fixed-cost commitment while the strategy is still being validated (Year 1–2); capabilities 1, 2, and 5 are more appropriately built in-house given their centrality to the core commercial/access strategy.
