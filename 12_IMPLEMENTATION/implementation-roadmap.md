## Cross-Cutting Enablers (All Years)
- Governance: monthly cross-functional review, quarterly executive review (see `governance-model.md`)
- KPI tracking against the framework in `KPI-framework.md`
- Continuous risk monitoring against `13_RISK_ANALYSIS/` register, with explicit review gates at each year-end

## Dependency Notes
- Tier 3+/rural commercial investment is explicitly **gated** on cold-chain infrastructure completion (see `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md`) — the roadmap deliberately avoids demand-generation spend in geographies that cannot yet reliably receive product.
- Ecosystem-layer investment (digital, provider training at scale) is gated on Year 1 segmented-pricing execution proving out (see `year-2-plan.md` review gate) — capital is not committed to the full Option D build before the Option C foundation is validated.
