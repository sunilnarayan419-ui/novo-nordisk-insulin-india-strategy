# Governance Model

## Structure
- **Executive Steering Committee** (quarterly): India country leadership + relevant global/regional stakeholders; reviews strategy execution against the financial model scenarios and approves major investment gates (e.g., Year 2 ecosystem-build funding, Year 3+ manufacturing review).
- **Cross-Functional Working Group** (monthly): commercial, market access, medical affairs, supply chain, and finance leads; tracks KPI dashboard (see `KPI-framework.md`) and manages day-to-day execution coordination.
- **Pricing Governance Sub-Committee** (as needed, minimum quarterly): dedicated oversight of the channel-differentiated pricing architecture to prevent leakage/arbitrage and to manage responses to any new NPPA/DPCO notifications affecting the portfolio.

## Decision Rights
- Country/India leadership owns strategy execution decisions within the approved investment envelope.
- Escalation to global/regional leadership required for: major new capital investment (e.g., local manufacturing decision in Year 3+), material scope changes to the strategy (e.g., abandoning the ecosystem build), and response to a major external shock (e.g., a compulsory-licensing action).

## Review Cadence and Triggers
Aligned to `00_PROJECT_CHARTER/decision-framework.md` escalation triggers: any of (a) a new compulsory-license/drastic price-control action on insulin, (b) a >5-point competitor share shift in two consecutive years, or (c) GLP-1 substitution measurably reducing insulin-eligible Type 2 population growth, triggers an out-of-cycle Executive Steering Committee review.
