# 0–12 Month Plan

## Quarter 1
- Finalize channel-differentiated pricing architecture and internal governance controls to prevent channel leakage
- Launch PMBJP distribution partnership discussions (Department of Pharmaceuticals engagement)
- Begin stock-out reduction program (demand forecasting, VMI pilot with 2–3 key institutional partners)

## Quarter 2
- Roll out three-tier portfolio packaging/positioning in 3–5 priority states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, and similar high-prevalence, mid-income states)
- Launch Type 1 diabetes patient-support program (co-pay assistance + NGO partnership) nationally
- Pilot cold-chain-compliant e-pharmacy partnership in Tier 1/2 cities

## Quarter 3
- Expand institutional volume-contract negotiations with private hospital networks
- Begin insurer/TPA pilot partnership discussions (outpatient insulin/monitoring benefit)
- Physician education program launch (simplified initiation protocols) targeting general physicians in Tier 2 cities

## Quarter 4
- Review Year 1 KPIs (see `KPI-framework.md`) against Base Case model targets
- Begin geography-prioritized cold-chain infrastructure investment planning for Tier 3+ expansion (Year 2)
- Finalize Year 2 ecosystem-build roadmap (digital adherence tool scoping, provider-training program design)

## Governance
Monthly cross-functional review (commercial, market access, supply chain, medical affairs) against the KPI framework; quarterly executive review against the financial model scenarios.
