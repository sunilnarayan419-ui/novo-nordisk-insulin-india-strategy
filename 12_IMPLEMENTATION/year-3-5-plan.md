# Year 3–5 Plan

## Focus: Full Ecosystem Maturity and National Scale
1. **National geographic coverage**: complete Tier 3+/rural cold-chain and distribution rollout in all priority states identified in Year 1–2; reassess remaining lower-priority geographies based on demonstrated ROI.
2. **Digital ecosystem maturity**: scale the digital adherence tool nationally, integrating with e-pharmacy refill/reorder workflows and, where feasible, basic telehealth/teleconsultation support for Tier 2/3 patients with limited specialist access.
3. **Reimbursement/insurance advocacy**: pursue sustained policy engagement (individually and via industry bodies such as OPPI) for an outpatient chronic-disease benefit within AB PM-JAY or as a private-insurance product standard, building on the evidence generated by Year 1–2 insurer pilot partnerships (see `07_MARKET_ACCESS/reimbursement-landscape.md`).
4. **Manufacturing/local investment review**: if volume growth is tracking toward the Upside Case, formally evaluate local (India-based) manufacturing or fill-finish investment for cost and supply-security benefits (see `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/manufacturing.md`).
5. **Continuous portfolio refresh**: proactively plan pricing/access strategy for the *next* generation of analogue products before they face the NLEM-expansion dynamic experienced with insulin glargine (see `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/lifecycle-analysis.md`) — i.e., build anticipatory, not reactive, price-control scenario planning into the new-product launch process.

## End-State Target (Year 5)
A fully realized Option D ecosystem: segmented, disciplined pricing; national PMBJP and cold-chain partnership; scaled digital adherence tools; mature institutional/insurer partnerships; and a demonstrated track record that supports continued favorable government/regulatory relationships — delivering the Upside Case financial outcome (`10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/README.md`).
