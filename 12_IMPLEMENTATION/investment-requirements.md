## Return Context
Against the Base Case model's projected Year-5 gross margin of ~₹1,315 crore/year (`10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/README.md`) and the Upside Case's ~₹1,806 crore/year, the illustrative cumulative 5-year investment of roughly ₹200–340 crore (excluding contingent manufacturing) represents a modest fraction of a single year's projected gross margin at the Upside Case run-rate — supporting the view that this is a financially proportionate investment relative to the targeted outcome, though actual capital allocation decisions should be validated against Novo Nordisk's real internal cost base before commitment.
