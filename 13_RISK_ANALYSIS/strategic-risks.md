# Strategic Risks

## S1 — Strategy Requires Sustained Multi-Year Commitment in a Possibly Impatient Organization
**Description**: The recommended phased approach (Option C now, Option D by Year 5) requires sustained investment and patience through a multi-year build; organizational or leadership change could disrupt this before full value is realized.
**Likelihood**: Moderate (a generic but real risk for any multi-year strategic transformation).
**Impact**: High if it materializes — a half-executed ecosystem strategy captures less of the differentiation value than either a fully executed strategy or a clean, well-executed segmented-only (Option C) approach.
**Mitigation**: Structure the roadmap with genuine value delivered at each phase gate (Option C alone should already show most of the Base Case's near-term revenue benefit), so that even a partial/interrupted execution still delivers meaningful value rather than requiring "all five years or nothing."

## S2 — Global Corporate Strategic Attention Shifts Away from Insulin
**Description**: As Novo Nordisk's global corporate strategy increasingly emphasizes GLP-1/obesity therapeutics, India insulin strategy could receive comparatively less internal investment priority/attention than this report assumes.
**Likelihood**: Moderate — a real dynamic already visible in how this report characterizes competitor strategic shifts (`04_COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE/`).
**Impact**: High if it results in under-resourcing the recommended strategy.
**Mitigation**: The financial case in `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/` and the investment-return framing in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/investment-requirements.md` are explicitly built to make the case for continued India insulin investment on its own economic merits, independent of relative internal prioritization against the GLP-1 franchise.

## S3 — Strategy Is Overtaken by a Structural Market Shift (e.g., Rapid Oral Insulin or Other Disruptive Technology)
**Description**: A disruptive technology (e.g., a clinically validated oral insulin formulation, or a dramatically cheaper long-acting delivery technology) could reshape the competitive landscape faster than this report's 5-year horizon assumes.
**Likelihood**: Low-to-moderate within this specific 5-year window, based on current global pipeline visibility, but non-zero.
**Impact**: Potentially high if it occurs.
**Mitigation**: Maintain the annual full-model recalibration discipline (`12_IMPLEMENTATION/KPI-framework.md`) to detect and respond to emerging technology shifts rather than assuming the current competitive/technology landscape is static for the full horizon.
