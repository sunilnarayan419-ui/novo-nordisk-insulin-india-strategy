# Regulatory Risks

## R1 — Further NLEM/DPCO Price-Control Expansion
**Description**: Additional analogue insulin categories (beyond glargine) could be added to NLEM, or ceiling prices could be tightened further, compressing margin on currently uncontrolled premium SKUs.
**Likelihood**: Moderate-to-high over a 5-year horizon, given the precedent set by the 2022 glargine addition and continued government affordability focus.
**Impact**: High — this is the single largest downside driver in the financial model (`10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/sensitivity-analysis/`).
**Mitigation**: Proactive pricing/access strategy for next-generation products (see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/year-3-5-plan.md`); visible access investment to reduce political pressure for punitive price action; scenario-tested portfolio economics that remain viable even if further NLEM expansion occurs (see Downside Case, `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/`).

## R2 — Compulsory Licensing / Extraordinary Government Action
**Description**: A low-probability but high-impact scenario where government invokes compulsory licensing or similarly extraordinary measures on a patented insulin product, citing public health necessity.
**Likelihood**: Low, but not zero, particularly if affordability advocacy intensifies without visible industry action.
**Impact**: Severe if it occurs, though narrow in scope (typically product-specific).
**Mitigation**: Sustained, credible access investment (Option D ecosystem build) is the most effective long-term mitigation, as it reduces the political/public-health rationale for such action.

## R3 — Import/Regulatory Approval Delays for Next-Generation Products
**Description**: CDSCO approval timelines for newer analogue or combination products could delay portfolio refresh, extending reliance on products more exposed to price-control/biosimilar pressure.
**Likelihood**: Moderate (regulatory timelines are a general industry risk in India, not insulin-specific).
**Impact**: Moderate.
**Mitigation**: Early and proactive regulatory engagement/filing strategy; portfolio planning that does not assume fastest-case approval timelines.

## R4 — Data Privacy / Digital Health Regulation
**Description**: As the ecosystem strategy incorporates digital adherence tools, evolving India data-protection regulation (e.g., under the Digital Personal Data Protection framework) could affect design and rollout timelines for health-data-handling digital products.
**Likelihood**: Moderate (regulatory framework is still maturing).
**Impact**: Low–moderate (primarily a timeline/design-compliance risk, not a strategy-invalidating one).
**Mitigation**: Privacy-by-design approach for digital tools; legal/compliance review built into the digital capability build (see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/capability-requirements.md`).
