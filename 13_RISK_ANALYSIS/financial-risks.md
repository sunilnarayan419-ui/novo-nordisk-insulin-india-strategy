# Financial Risks

## F1 — Margin Compression Faster/Deeper Than Modeled
**Description**: Combined effect of price control expansion and biosimilar competition compresses margin faster than the Downside Case assumes.
**Likelihood**: Low-to-moderate (Downside Case is intended to be a genuinely conservative stress test, but cannot rule out a more severe outcome).
**Impact**: High.
**Mitigation**: Maintain portfolio mix flexibility (ability to shift commercial emphasis toward whichever tier remains most defensible) and cost discipline in access-program spend (ensure programs are targeted, not blanket, per `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/access-vs-margin-tradeoff.md`).

## F2 — Currency/Exchange-Rate Volatility
**Description**: INR/USD movement affects reported USD-equivalent results and potentially input costs if API or components are imported.
**Likelihood**: Ongoing, inherent risk.
**Impact**: Low-to-moderate on strategic decision-making (the model's core INR-denominated conclusions are not currency-dependent; only USD-equivalent reporting is affected).
**Mitigation**: Report and plan primarily in INR terms for India-specific decision-making; treat USD figures as supplementary translation only (per `00_PROJECT_CHARTER/assumptions-register.md`, A8).

## F3 — Investment Overrun in Ecosystem Build
**Description**: Digital tool development, provider training scale-up, or cold-chain infrastructure investment costs exceed the illustrative ranges in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/investment-requirements.md`.
**Likelihood**: Moderate (common risk in any multi-year capability-build program).
**Impact**: Moderate (the phased, gated investment approach in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/implementation-roadmap.md` is specifically designed to limit this risk by not committing full-scope capital before Year 1 results validate the approach).
**Mitigation**: Maintain the year-over-year review-gate discipline described in the implementation roadmap; prefer partner-based delivery for higher-uncertainty capabilities (digital, patient support) over full in-house build, per `12_IMPLEMENTATION/capability-requirements.md`.

## F4 — Working Capital Strain from Rural Distribution Support
**Description**: Extending buffer-stock financing/credit support to smaller rural retailers (per `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/availability-barriers.md`) ties up working capital with uncertain near-term return.
**Likelihood**: Moderate.
**Impact**: Low-to-moderate (bounded by the scale of the pilot-first approach recommended in the implementation roadmap).
**Mitigation**: Pilot at limited scale before broader commitment; consider partnering with existing rural distribution-finance intermediaries rather than extending credit directly.
