# Competitive Risks

## C1 — Accelerated Biosimilar Share Gains
**Description**: Biocon Biologics or another domestic/biosimilar manufacturer achieves faster-than-modeled share gains in analogue insulin, particularly in the mainstream private channel where Novo Nordisk's segmented strategy is targeting growth.
**Likelihood**: Moderate-to-high, consistent with the structural cost advantage discussed in `04_COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE/competitive-threats.md`.
**Impact**: High — directly tested in the model's "Novo Nordisk market share" and "Competitor pricing" sensitivity variables (`10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/sensitivity-analysis/`).
**Mitigation**: Differentiate on device/ecosystem value rather than compete purely on price; monitor biosimilar approval and tender-win activity closely as an early warning indicator (KPI tracking, `12_IMPLEMENTATION/KPI-framework.md`).

## C2 — GLP-1 Substitution Faster Than Modeled
**Description**: GLP-1 receptor agonist adoption in the Type 2 population grows faster than the Base Case assumption, reducing insulin-eligible population growth, particularly in the premium/private channel.
**Likelihood**: Moderate, and rising over the horizon as GLP-1 access broadens.
**Impact**: Moderate-to-high on the premium-tier revenue specifically; lower impact on the access/mainstream tiers where insulin remains the standard of care for longer.
**Mitigation**: Diversify commercial focus across tiers (already built into the segmented strategy) rather than over-relying on premium-tier analogue growth; monitor GLP-1 market data as a leading indicator.

## C3 — New Entrant Competition
**Description**: An additional international biosimilar/generic manufacturer enters the India insulin market, further fragmenting competitive dynamics.
**Likelihood**: Moderate.
**Impact**: Low–moderate (India is already a fragmented market; incremental entrants have diminishing marginal effect on an already-competitive structure).
**Mitigation**: Continue building the ecosystem differentiation that is harder for any single new entrant to replicate quickly.

## C4 — Domestic Manufacturer Institutional Relationship Advantage Widens
**Description**: Domestic manufacturers deepen state-government tender relationships further, structurally reducing Novo Nordisk's institutional-channel access over time.
**Likelihood**: Moderate.
**Impact**: Moderate (this channel is already a lower-priority focus in the recommended strategy — see `07_MARKET_ACCESS/institutional-procurement.md` — limiting incremental downside).
**Mitigation**: Maintain selective institutional presence for relationship/access-narrative purposes rather than fully exiting the channel, even if it is not the primary margin source.
