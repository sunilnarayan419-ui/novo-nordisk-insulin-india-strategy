# Operational Risks

## O1 — Cold-Chain Investment Underdelivers on Expected Volume Recovery
**Description**: Targeted cold-chain infrastructure investment in priority Tier 3+ geographies (`09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md`) does not translate into the modeled volume recovery, e.g., due to persistent demand-side barriers (affordability, awareness) that cold-chain fixes alone don't resolve.
**Likelihood**: Moderate — this is a real risk given that availability is only one of several compounding barriers (see `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/adoption-barriers.md`).
**Impact**: Moderate.
**Mitigation**: Sequence cold-chain investment with, not ahead of, complementary demand-side interventions (patient education, affordability programs) in the same target geographies, rather than treating supply-chain fix as sufficient alone.

## O2 — Channel Pricing Leakage/Arbitrage
**Description**: Products priced for the access tier are diverted/resold into the mainstream or premium channel (or vice versa), undermining the segmented pricing architecture's integrity.
**Likelihood**: Moderate — a known risk in any differentiated-pricing model, especially across a large, fragmented distribution network.
**Impact**: Moderate-to-high if unaddressed (directly undermines the core mechanism of the recommended strategy).
**Mitigation**: Distinct SKU/packaging identifiers by tier, distribution-partner contractual controls, and the dedicated pricing governance sub-committee described in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/governance-model.md`.

## O3 — Capability/Talent Gap in New Functions
**Description**: Difficulty hiring/building the market-access, digital, and health-economics capabilities identified as gaps in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/capability-requirements.md`, slowing ecosystem-build execution.
**Likelihood**: Moderate (specialized talent in these areas is competitive across the pharma/health-tech industry in India).
**Impact**: Moderate (primarily a timeline risk rather than a strategy-invalidating one).
**Mitigation**: Partner-first approach for the highest-gap capabilities (digital, patient support) to reduce dependency on immediate in-house hiring success.

## O4 — Program Fragmentation Across Multiple Workstreams
**Description**: The ecosystem strategy involves many parallel workstreams (pricing, distribution, digital, provider training, insurer partnerships) — risk of poor coordination diluting overall impact.
**Likelihood**: Moderate, particularly at Year 2–3 scale-up.
**Impact**: Moderate.
**Mitigation**: The cross-functional governance structure in `12_IMPLEMENTATION/governance-model.md` is specifically designed to address this; monthly working-group cadence is the primary control.
