# Scope & Boundaries

## In Scope
- India insulin market: human insulin (regular, NPH, premix) and analogue insulin (rapid-, long-, and mixed-acting), delivery devices (vials, cartridges/pens, prefilled pens)
- Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes populations requiring insulin (Type 2 insulin-requiring/insulin-intensified segment is the larger commercial opportunity; Type 1 is the higher-acuity, insulin-dependent-from-diagnosis segment)
- Public and private channels: government procurement/tenders, Ayushman Bharat–linked hospitals, PMBJP/Jan Aushadhi, private hospitals, retail pharmacy, e-pharmacy
- 3–5 year strategic horizon (FY2026–FY2031 equivalent)
- India geography, with sub-national segmentation (state-tier, urban/rural, healthcare-infrastructure maturity)

## Out of Scope
- Other diabetes drug classes (metformin, SGLT2i, DPP-4i, GLP-1 receptor agonists) except as competitive/substitution context
- Novo Nordisk's obesity, hemophilia, or growth-hormone franchises
- Manufacturing-site-level engineering detail (supply chain is treated at a strategic/network level, not a plant-engineering level)
- Country markets outside India
- Non-public financial data, confidential contracts, or proprietary Novo Nordisk pricing/rebate terms (see `DISCLAIMER.md`)

## Boundary Conditions on the Analysis
- All market sizing uses top-down triangulation from public epidemiological studies (ICMR-INDIAB), IDF Diabetes Atlas, NPPA/DPCO pricing notifications, and public market-research summaries — not primary data collection.
- Financial figures for Novo Nordisk India specifically (as opposed to global filings) are frequently not separately disclosed; where this is the case, the model uses documented consulting-assumption ranges, clearly flagged.
- The analysis assumes no major regulatory discontinuity (e.g., a sudden compulsory-licensing action on insulin) beyond what is already signaled in current policy.
