# Project Charter — Strategic Assessment of Insulin Access, Affordability, and Portfolio Competitiveness in India

## Client
Novo Nordisk (case-study client; this is an independent, publicly-sourced consulting exercise — see `DISCLAIMER.md`)

## Sponsor (simulated)
Novo Nordisk India — Managing Director / Country General Manager

## Engagement Type
Life sciences / biotech commercial strategy — market access, pricing, portfolio, and operations

## Core Business Question
How should Novo Nordisk optimize its insulin portfolio, pricing/access strategy, and market approach in India over the next 3–5 years to increase appropriate insulin adoption and patient access while protecting sustainable revenue, margins, and competitive position?

## Duration
12-week equivalent engagement structure (compressed for case-study purposes into the phased workplan in `01_PROJECT_MANAGEMENT/workplan.md`)

## Deliverables
1. Full strategy report (20 sections, per `problem-statement.md`)
2. Quantitative decision model with 3 scenarios + sensitivity analysis (`10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/`)
3. Strategic options evaluation and recommendation (`11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/`)
4. Implementation roadmap and KPI framework (`12_IMPLEMENTATION/`)
5. Supporting exhibits and diagrams (`16_EXHIBITS/`)

## Team Structure (simulated)
- Engagement Lead — overall narrative, recommendation, executive materials
- Market & Competitive Analyst — sections 02–04
- Health Economics & Access Analyst — sections 06–08
- Supply Chain Analyst — section 09
- Financial Modeler — section 10
- QA / Fact-Checker — section 19

## Governing Principles
- **Evidence-first**: every material claim traces to a labeled source category (FACT / DATA-BASED INFERENCE / CONSULTING ASSUMPTION / SCENARIO ASSUMPTION / STRATEGIC JUDGMENT — see `18_RESEARCH/source-library.md`).
- **Public-information only**: no claim of access to Novo Nordisk's internal, confidential, or proprietary data (see `DISCLAIMER.md`).
- **Executive-usable**: every analytical section closes with an implication for the recommendation, not analysis for its own sake.

## Success Criteria
The engagement is complete when an executive reader can answer, from this repository alone: what to do, why, for whom, where, how much to invest, what the economic upside is, what could go wrong, and how success will be measured (per `problem-statement.md`).

## Sign-off
This charter frames a self-directed case study completed for skills demonstration (see `20_CAREER_PROOF/`), not a live client engagement.
