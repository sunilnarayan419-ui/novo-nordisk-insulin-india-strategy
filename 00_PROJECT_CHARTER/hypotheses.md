# Working Hypotheses

Structured as an issue tree of testable hypotheses (see `issue-tree.md` for the full tree). Each is labeled with its evidentiary status as of report completion.

**H1 — Market**: India's insulin-treated population is growing faster than diagnosed-diabetes prevalence because of rising diagnosis rates and earlier insulin initiation in Type 2 diabetes.
*Status: DATA-BASED INFERENCE — supported by ICMR-INDIAB prevalence trends and low current diagnosis/treatment-control rates (see `02_INDUSTRY_LANDSCAPE/diabetes-landscape.md`).*

**H2 — Affordability is the binding constraint, not clinical awareness**: For the majority of insulin-requiring patients outside metro private care, out-of-pocket price — not lack of physician awareness — is the primary barrier to initiating or persisting on modern analogues.
*Status: DATA-BASED INFERENCE, supported by NPPA/DPCO price-differential data and out-of-pocket expenditure literature (see `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/`).*

**H3 — A single national price will not maximize access or revenue**: Because affordability varies 5–10x across payer channels, a segmented pricing/access architecture outperforms a single price point on both patient reach and portfolio economics.
*Status: CONSULTING ASSUMPTION, grounded in differential pricing precedent (Jan Aushadhi generics, tiered pricing in other LMICs) — see `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/access-vs-margin-tradeoff.md`.*

**H4 — Human insulin remains commercially and clinically relevant**: For a large share of Indian Type 2 patients, well-managed human insulin delivers acceptable outcomes at a fraction of analogue cost; ceding this segment entirely to biosimilar/domestic competitors sacrifices volume and market-access leverage.
*Status: STRATEGIC JUDGMENT, informed by clinical-equivalence literature in non-hypoglycemia-prone patients and biosimilar cost data.*

**H5 — Supply chain, not manufacturing capacity, is the more tractable near-term lever**: Cold-chain and last-mile distribution gaps in Tier 3+ towns and rural India destroy more addressable volume than global manufacturing constraints.
*Status: DATA-BASED INFERENCE — see `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md`.*

**H6 — An ecosystem/access play compounds advantage over a pure-pricing play**: Bundling insulin with patient support, glucose monitoring, and provider training produces more durable adherence and defensibility than price competition alone, but requires more capital and time to scale.
*Status: STRATEGIC JUDGMENT — tested in `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/option-D-ecosystem-strategy.md`.*
