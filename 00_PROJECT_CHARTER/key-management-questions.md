# Key Management Questions (KMQs)

This engagement is structured around the KMQs below (restated from `problem-statement.md`), each mapped to the repository section that answers it.

| # | Question | Answered in |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the current/projected addressable insulin market in India, and which segments carry the largest unmet need? | `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/` |
| 2 | How do insulin categories compare on clinical value, convenience, and adherence, and how should this be turned into an economic value proposition? | `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/`, `08_HEALTH_ECONOMICS/` |
| 3 | How does affordability vary by segment, and what pricing/access levers improve penetration without destroying economics? | `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/` |
| 4 | Who are the competitors, how do they differ, and where is Novo Nordisk defensible vs. vulnerable? | `04_COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE/` |
| 5 | Should the portfolio prioritize premium analogues, human insulin, affordability offers, or a segmented approach? | `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/`, `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/` |
| 6 | What role do government programs, insurance, and institutions play, and what market-access strategy improves adoption? | `07_MARKET_ACCESS/` |
| 7 | What supply-chain factors affect availability/affordability, and where can operational change create value? | `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/` |
| 8 | What are the four+ strategic options and how do they compare? | `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/` |
| 9 | What is the final recommendation — what, where, for whom, how, at what cost, for what return? | `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/recommended-strategy.md`, `17_CONSULTING_DELIVERABLES/` |
| 10 | What does the quantitative model show under base/upside/downside cases and sensitivity analysis? | `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/` |

## Decision-Maker Lens
Every KMQ answer is written to support one decision-maker question: *"Where should Novo Nordisk India place its next incremental rupee of commercial investment, and what return — in patients reached and margin protected — should it expect?"*
