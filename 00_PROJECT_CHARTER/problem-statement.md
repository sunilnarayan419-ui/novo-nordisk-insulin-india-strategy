PROJECT TYPE:
Life Sciences / Biotechnology Strategy Consulting Case

CASE STUDY CLIENT:
Novo Nordisk

PROJECT TITLE:
Strategic Assessment of Insulin Access, Affordability, and Portfolio Competitiveness in India

CLIENT CONTEXT:
Novo Nordisk is a global biopharmaceutical company with a major presence in diabetes care and a portfolio spanning human insulin, modern insulin analogues, and other diabetes therapies.

India represents a strategically important diabetes market characterized by a large patient population, significant variation in healthcare affordability, heterogeneous treatment access, strong price sensitivity, multiple distribution channels, and competition from both multinational and domestic pharmaceutical companies.

The commercial challenge is therefore not simply whether insulin is clinically effective. The strategic question is how Novo Nordisk can sustainably expand access to appropriate insulin therapy while maintaining commercial viability and competitive positioning.

CORE BUSINESS PROBLEM:

How should Novo Nordisk optimize its insulin portfolio, pricing/access strategy, and market approach in India over the next 3–5 years to increase appropriate insulin adoption and patient access while protecting sustainable revenue, margins, and competitive position?

KEY MANAGEMENT QUESTIONS:

1. MARKET ATTRACTIVENESS
   - What is the current and projected addressable insulin market in India?
   - Which patient segments represent the largest unmet need?
   - Where are the major barriers to insulin initiation and continuation?
   - Which geographic, socioeconomic, and healthcare-system segments offer the greatest strategic opportunity?

2. PATIENT & CLINICAL VALUE
   - How do different insulin categories compare in terms of clinical value, convenience, adherence, safety, and treatment outcomes?
   - Which patient segments derive the greatest incremental value from modern insulin analogues versus lower-cost alternatives?
   - How should clinical value be translated into an economic/value proposition for payers, physicians, and patients?

3. PRICING & AFFORDABILITY
   - How does insulin affordability vary across relevant Indian patient segments?
   - What is the relationship between insulin price, treatment persistence, and access?
   - Could differentiated pricing, pack sizes, patient-support mechanisms, or access programs improve market penetration without destroying portfolio economics?
   - What price/access trade-offs exist between volume expansion and margin preservation?

4. COMPETITIVE STRATEGY
   - Who are Novo Nordisk's major competitors across relevant insulin categories?
   - How do competitors differ in price, product positioning, distribution, physician engagement, and patient access?
   - Where does Novo Nordisk possess a defensible competitive advantage?
   - Where is the portfolio vulnerable to commoditization or price competition?

5. PORTFOLIO STRATEGY
   - Should Novo Nordisk prioritize premium insulin analogues, human insulin, affordability-oriented offerings, or a segmented portfolio strategy?
   - Which products/categories should receive greater commercial investment?
   - Are there portfolio gaps that could constrain future growth?
   - How should the portfolio balance access, innovation, volume, and profitability?

6. MARKET ACCESS & HEALTH ECONOMICS
   - What role do government programs, public procurement, private insurance, hospitals, pharmacies, and out-of-pocket expenditure play in insulin access?
   - What evidence would be required to demonstrate the economic value of modern insulin therapies?
   - Under what circumstances could improved clinical outcomes or adherence offset higher acquisition costs?
   - What market-access strategy could improve reimbursement and institutional adoption?

7. SUPPLY CHAIN & OPERATIONS
   - What supply-chain factors influence insulin availability and affordability?
   - Where could stock-outs, cold-chain requirements, inventory economics, or distribution inefficiencies create commercial leakage?
   - Could changes in manufacturing, packaging, distribution, or inventory strategy improve accessibility without materially compromising economics?

8. STRATEGIC OPTIONS

   Develop and evaluate at least four strategic options:

   OPTION A:
   Premium/innovation-led strategy

   OPTION B:
   Access and affordability-led strategy

   OPTION C:
   Segmented portfolio + differentiated access strategy

   OPTION D:
   Ecosystem strategy integrating insulin, patient support, digital engagement, healthcare providers, and market-access partnerships

   For each option evaluate:

   - Patient impact
   - Clinical value
   - Market potential
   - Revenue potential
   - Margin implications
   - Investment requirements
   - Competitive response
   - Execution complexity
   - Regulatory/access constraints
   - Strategic sustainability

9. RECOMMENDATION

   Based on the analysis, determine:

   "What should Novo Nordisk do, where should it do it, for which patient segments, through which products/channels, and under what economic model?"

   The recommendation must identify:

   - Priority patient segments
   - Priority geographic/market segments
   - Priority insulin categories/products
   - Pricing/access approach
   - Market-access strategy
   - Commercial model
   - Operational requirements
   - Required investments
   - Expected financial impact
   - Key risks
   - Implementation timeline

10. DECISION MODEL

Build a quantitative strategic model capable of estimating:

   - Addressable patient population
   - Insulin-treated population
   - Market share
   - Treatment adoption
   - Treatment persistence/adherence assumptions
   - Annual treatment cost
   - Revenue
   - Gross-margin sensitivity
   - Patient affordability
   - Scenario-based market growth
   - ROI / investment attractiveness

Develop at least three scenarios:

   BASE CASE
   UPSIDE CASE
   DOWNSIDE CASE

Additionally perform sensitivity analysis on:

   - Insulin price
   - Market share
   - Patient adoption
   - Treatment persistence
   - Competitor pricing
   - Distribution cost
   - Access-program expenditure
   - Market growth

If you were advising Novo Nordisk's India leadership team, what specific strategic decision would you recommend regarding insulin access, portfolio positioning, pricing, and commercial investment—and what evidence demonstrates that this strategy creates superior value for patients and Novo Nordisk compared with the alternatives?
