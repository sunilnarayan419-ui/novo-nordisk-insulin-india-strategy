# Objectives

## Primary Objective
Determine the optimal 3–5 year strategy for Novo Nordisk's insulin business in India across portfolio, pricing/access, and commercial-operating-model dimensions, and quantify its expected financial and patient-access impact.

## Supporting Objectives
1. **Market sizing** — establish TAM/SAM/SOM for insulin-treated patients in India, segmented by diabetes type, treatment stage, and payer channel.
2. **Segment prioritization** — identify which patient segments (clinical, geographic, socioeconomic) offer the best combination of unmet need and commercial accessibility.
3. **Competitive positioning** — map Novo Nordisk's position against Eli Lilly, Sanofi, Biocon Biologics, Wockhardt, and other domestic players across price, product, and channel.
4. **Value articulation** — translate the clinical value of analogue insulins and delivery devices into an economic case usable with payers, hospitals, and physicians.
5. **Access/margin trade-off** — quantify how differentiated pricing, pack sizes, and patient-support programs affect volume, revenue, and margin.
6. **Operational levers** — identify supply-chain and cold-chain interventions that reduce commercial leakage (stock-outs, wastage) without materially raising cost-to-serve.
7. **Decision model** — build a scenario-based quantitative model (base/upside/downside) with sensitivity analysis on the eight variables specified in the problem statement.
8. **Recommendation** — issue one clear, defensible strategic recommendation with an implementation roadmap.

## Non-Objectives (see `scope-and-boundaries.md`)
- Global Novo Nordisk strategy (India-only scope)
- GLP-1 / obesity-care strategy (mentioned only where it materially interacts with insulin commercial infrastructure)
- Detailed regulatory/legal opinion (directional regulatory risk only)
- Any claim on non-public financial or operational data
