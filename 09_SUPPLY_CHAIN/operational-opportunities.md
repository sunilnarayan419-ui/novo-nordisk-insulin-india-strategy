# Operational Opportunities (Supply Chain Synthesis)

## Prioritized Opportunity List

| # | Opportunity | Impact | Effort/Cost | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Last-mile stock-out reduction (forecasting, VMI, buffer-stock support) | High — directly converts existing willing demand into fulfilled demand | Low–Moderate | **Immediate (Year 1)** |
| 2 | PMBJP cold-chain distribution partnership | High — leverages existing 18,000+ outlet network | Moderate | **Near-term (Year 1–2)** |
| 3 | Targeted rural/Tier 3 cold-chain infrastructure (solar/battery refrigeration) | High in targeted geographies, but not nationally scalable near-term | Moderate–High | **Phased (Year 2–3), geography-prioritized** |
| 4 | Cold-chain-compliant e-pharmacy partnership | Moderate–High in Tier 1/2, lower in deep rural | Low–Moderate | **Near-term (Year 1)** |
| 5 | Local manufacturing/fill-finish investment | Potentially high long-term, cost and supply-security benefit | High | **Longer-term consideration (Year 3–5)** |

## Sequencing Logic
Opportunities 1 and 4 are prioritized first because they require the least capital and can be implemented within existing commercial operations; Opportunity 2 is the highest-leverage access investment given PMBJP's scale and government alignment; Opportunity 3 should be geographically targeted to the priority states identified in `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/geographic-opportunity.md` rather than attempted nationally at once; Opportunity 5 is deferred pending confirmation that the access strategy is generating the volume growth that would justify it.

## Expected Impact on the Financial Model
The `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/` base case assumes a modest (low single-digit percentage) reduction in cold-chain/stock-out-related volume loss in targeted geographies by Year 3 as a result of Opportunities 1–3, contributing to the overall access-expansion volume growth assumption (see `00_PROJECT_CHARTER/assumptions-register.md`, A6).
