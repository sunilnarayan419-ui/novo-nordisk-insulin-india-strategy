# Availability Barriers (Stock-Outs & Access Gaps)

## Sources of Stock-Outs (DBI, general pharmaceutical supply-chain literature applied to insulin)
1. **Demand-forecasting mismatch** at the last-mile retail/institutional level, particularly in lower-volume rural outlets where a single stock-out can persist for weeks before reorder.
2. **Cold-chain-related spoilage** requiring stock disposal and reorder lead time (see `cold-chain.md`).
3. **Institutional procurement cycle gaps** — government tender-based procurement can create supply gaps between contract cycles if reorder timing is not tightly managed.
4. **Working-capital constraints at small independent pharmacies**, limiting their ability to hold buffer stock of a relatively higher-unit-cost, temperature-sensitive product like insulin compared to standard tablets.

## Consequence
Stock-outs convert *existing, willing, paying* demand into either (a) treatment interruption (with the clinical risks described in `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/affordability-analysis.md`), or (b) a forced brand/product switch to whatever is available — a direct commercial leakage mechanism distinct from price or awareness barriers.

## Mitigation Levers
1. Data-informed demand forecasting and safety-stock policy specifically for high-value, temperature-sensitive SKUs, rather than applying standard tablet/capsule inventory logic
2. Vendor-managed inventory (VMI) arrangements with key institutional and high-volume retail partners
3. Buffer-stock financing support (e.g., extended credit terms) for smaller rural pharmacies to reduce their working-capital barrier to holding adequate insulin stock
4. Real-time stock visibility tools (even simple SMS/app-based stock-level reporting) for last-mile outlets, enabling faster reorder triggering

## Strategic Implication
Addressing availability barriers is a **relatively low-capital, high-impact** lever compared to cold-chain infrastructure build-out, and can often be implemented faster — recommended as an early (Year 1) operational workstream (see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/0-12-month-plan.md`).
