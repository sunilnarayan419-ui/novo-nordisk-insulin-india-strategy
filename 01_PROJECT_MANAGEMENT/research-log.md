# Research Log

Chronological log of primary research queries and what they resolved. Full citation list in `18_RESEARCH/source-library.md`.

1. India diabetes prevalence → ICMR-INDIAB (2023, Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology): 11.4% weighted prevalence; ~101 million (10.1 crore) people with diabetes nationally (PIB, 2023).
2. Global insulin market structure → Novo Nordisk, Sanofi, Eli Lilly hold ~90% of global insulin market by value; India/LMIC volume share for smaller/biosimilar players is materially higher (~28% in India per IQVIA Institute).
3. India insulin pricing → NPPA/DPCO ceiling prices: intermediate-acting/premix human insulin (40 IU/ml) ≈ ₹15.09/ml (scheduled formulation); insulin glargine pen ≈ ₹244/ml ceiling (NLEM 2022 addition, effective 2023) — a >15x per-ml differential between price-controlled human insulin and a price-controlled analogue pen.
4. Government access programs → Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (~55 crore beneficiaries, hospitalization-focused); PMBJP/Jan Aushadhi (18,000+ Kendras, includes anti-diabetic generics, but outpatient/insulin access still limited relative to need).
5. Cold-chain/wastage → general LMIC literature suggests substantial cold-chain-related insulin wastage (order of magnitude 25–30% in some developing-region contexts); no India-insulin-specific public figure identified — treated as a consulting assumption range.

Searches were run through the engagement's completion date; see individual section files for in-line source attribution.
