# Workplan (12-week equivalent structure)

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Framing | 1 | Problem definition, issue tree, hypotheses | `00_PROJECT_CHARTER/` |
| 2. Landscape research | 2–3 | Disease burden, treatment landscape, healthcare system | `02_INDUSTRY_LANDSCAPE/` |
| 3. Market sizing & segmentation | 3–4 | TAM/SAM/SOM, patient segments, geography | `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/` |
| 4. Competitive & portfolio analysis | 4–5 | Competitor mapping, product/portfolio gap analysis | `04_`, `05_` |
| 5. Pricing, access & health economics | 5–7 | Affordability, market access, value framework | `06_`, `07_`, `08_` |
| 6. Supply chain diagnostic | 6–7 | Distribution, cold chain, inventory economics | `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/` |
| 7. Quantitative modeling | 7–9 | Base/upside/downside model, sensitivities | `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/` |
| 8. Strategic options | 9–10 | Four options, scorecard, recommendation | `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/` |
| 9. Implementation & risk | 10–11 | Roadmap, governance, KPIs, risk register | `12_`, `13_` |
| 10. Synthesis & QA | 11–12 | Executive summary, fact-check, final review | `17_`, `19_` |

## Methodology Notes
Top-down + triangulated sizing (epidemiology × treatment-rate assumptions × price bands), cross-checked against public market-research summaries where available. See `18_RESEARCH/source-library.md` for full source list.
