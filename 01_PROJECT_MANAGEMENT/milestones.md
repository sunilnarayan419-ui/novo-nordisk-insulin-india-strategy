# Milestones

| Milestone | Target | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| M1 — Charter approved | Week 1 | Problem statement, objectives, scope signed off |
| M2 — Market sizing validated | Week 4 | TAM/SAM/SOM with triangulated sources |
| M3 — Competitive & portfolio view complete | Week 5 | Competitor profiles, portfolio gap map |
| M4 — Access & economics case built | Week 7 | Affordability analysis, value framework |
| M5 — Financial model locked | Week 9 | Base/upside/downside scenarios + 8-variable sensitivity |
| M6 — Strategic option selected | Week 10 | Scorecard + recommendation memo |
| M7 — Final report issued | Week 12 | Full 20-section report + executive summary |

## Implementation-phase milestones (post-recommendation, see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/`)
- 0–12 months: pilot segmented pricing/access architecture in 3–5 priority states
- Year 2: scale market-access partnerships and patient-support program nationally
- Year 3–5: full ecosystem rollout and portfolio-mix targets achieved
