## D. Glossary of Evidence Labels
- **FACT** — directly verifiable from a cited public source
- **DATA-BASED INFERENCE (DBI)** — a reasonable inference drawn from cited data, not stated verbatim in any single source
- **CONSULTING ASSUMPTION (CA)** — an analytical assumption made to enable modeling/decision-making where public data does not exist at the needed granularity, clearly bounded and stated
- **SCENARIO ASSUMPTION (SA)** — an assumption specific to one of the three financial-model scenarios (Base/Upside/Downside)
- **STRATEGIC JUDGMENT** — the report's own analytical/strategic conclusion, presented as a recommendation rather than a fact
