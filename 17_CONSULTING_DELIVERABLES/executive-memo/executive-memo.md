# Executive Memo

**To**: Novo Nordisk India Leadership Team (simulated recipient, case-study exercise)
**From**: Independent Strategy Analysis
**Re**: Insulin Access, Affordability, and Portfolio Competitiveness Strategy — Recommendation

## Bottom Line
Adopt a segmented pricing and portfolio strategy immediately, with a phased build toward an integrated patient-ecosystem model over five years. This is the only one of four evaluated strategic options that credibly satisfies both halves of the mandate — expand access, protect economics — with a quantified, risk-tested financial case.

## Why Not the Alternatives
- A premium-only strategy defends near-term margin but cedes the majority of the price-sensitive market and conflicts directly with the access mandate.
- A pure price-led access strategy risks a margin race-to-the-bottom against India-based biosimilar manufacturers with a structurally lower cost base — financially fragile, not sustainable.
- A full ecosystem build attempted immediately is directionally the right long-term end-state but carries the highest execution complexity and investment requirement of any option — better reached in phases than attempted as a first move.

## What This Requires From Leadership
1. Approval of channel-differentiated pricing, with the internal governance discipline to prevent leakage between tiers
2. Sustained multi-year investment commitment (~₹200–340 crore illustrative over 5 years, excluding any manufacturing decision), released in gated phases tied to demonstrated results
3. New capability investment in market-access, digital health, and health-economics functions — largely partner-first in Year 1–2 rather than immediate full in-house build
4. Proactive, not reactive, engagement with government on pricing policy and outpatient reimbursement — the single largest downside risk (further price control) is best managed through visible, credible access investment, not defensive lobbying alone

Base Case: ~₹2,083 crore revenue and ~63% gross margin by Year 5, with continued patient-volume growth even in the Downside Case. Full detail in `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/`.
