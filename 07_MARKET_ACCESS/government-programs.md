# Government Programs

## Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) (FACT)
- Launched 2018; provides cashless hospitalization coverage up to ₹5 lakh/family/year for an estimated **~55 crore beneficiaries** (bottom ~40–50% of India's population by the SECC 2011 database), recently extended to cover all citizens aged 70+ regardless of income.
- **Design implication**: PM-JAY is structured around **secondary/tertiary hospitalization** (covering ~1,929 listed procedures) — it is not designed to reimburse routine, self-administered outpatient chronic medication like daily insulin. Diabetes-related PM-JAY utilization is therefore concentrated in acute complications (DKA admission, cardiovascular events, amputation, dialysis) rather than in routine insulin supply.

## Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) / Jan Aushadhi (FACT)
- Runs **18,000+ Jan Aushadhi Kendras** nationally (Department of Pharmaceuticals), stocking **2,110+ generic medicines** across 29 therapeutic categories including anti-diabetics, at prices significantly below branded MRP.
- By mid-2025, cumulative Jan Aushadhi sales reached an MRP-equivalent value of ~₹7,700 crore, generating an estimated ~₹38,000 crore in consumer savings versus branded alternatives (IBEF, citing government data) — evidence of both meaningful scale and a strong government commitment to this channel.
- **Relevance to insulin**: PMBJP is a credible, scaling channel for affordable human insulin distribution, though consistency of insulin-specific stock (a cold-chain-sensitive product, unlike most PMBJP tablet/capsule SKUs) across all 18,000+ outlets is a distinct operational challenge (see `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md`).

## National Programme for Prevention and Control of Non-Communicable Diseases (NP-NCD) (FACT)
- Government technical/financial support to states for NCD screening and management, with diabetes as a core component (PIB, 2023) — this program is the primary lever for expanding the *diagnosed* base, upstream of insulin demand.

## NPPA / DPCO (Price Regulation) (FACT)
- Covered in depth in `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/pricing-landscape.md`; functions as both an access lever (keeps human insulin affordable) and a margin constraint (increasingly extends to analogues).

## Strategic Implication
Novo Nordisk's most effective near-term government engagement is likely **PMBJP-channel partnership for human insulin distribution** and **state-level NCD screening program collaboration** (to grow the diagnosed base) — direct AB PM-JAY engagement is structurally limited by that scheme's hospitalization focus, though advocacy for an outpatient chronic-disease rider is a longer-term, higher-uncertainty policy opportunity worth monitoring.
