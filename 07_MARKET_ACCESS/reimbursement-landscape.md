# Reimbursement Landscape

## Current State (FACT/DBI)
- Outpatient chronic medication, including insulin, is **largely not reimbursed** by either government schemes (AB PM-JAY's hospitalization focus) or the majority of private health insurance policies in India, which historically have emphasized hospitalization/inpatient coverage over outpatient/pharmacy benefits.
- A minority of employer-sponsored or premium private insurance plans include outpatient/OPD riders that may cover chronic medication including insulin, but this remains a small share of the insured population, which is itself a minority of India's total population.
- PMBJP functions as a de facto affordability mechanism (very low prices) rather than formal "reimbursement," but achieves a similar patient-affordability outcome for the SKUs it stocks.

## Direction of Travel (DBI)
- There is a slow but visible trend toward outpatient/OPD benefit expansion in India's private insurance market, driven by rising chronic-disease prevalence and insurer product innovation (e.g., some newer health insurance products marketed with OPD/wellness riders) — a trend Novo Nordisk should monitor and potentially support through insurer partnerships, since expanded outpatient reimbursement would directly grow the addressable, price-insensitive segment of the insulin market.

## Policy Opportunity (STRATEGIC JUDGMENT)
Advocating (individually or via industry bodies such as OPPI) for a defined **chronic-disease outpatient benefit** — potentially structured as a rider to AB PM-JAY or as a mandated minimum outpatient benefit in private insurance — would be a higher-leverage, longer-horizon lever for expanding sustainable insulin access than price competition alone, though it requires sustained multi-year policy engagement and is not a near-term financial-model input (treated as an upside-case qualitative driver, not quantified in the base case).

## Strategic Implication
In the absence of near-term reimbursement expansion, manufacturer-funded patient-support programs are the most practical near-term substitute for formal reimbursement (see `08_HEALTH_ECONOMICS/value-proposition.md` and `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/option-D-ecosystem-strategy.md`).
