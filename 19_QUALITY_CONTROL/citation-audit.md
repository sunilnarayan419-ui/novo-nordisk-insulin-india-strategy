# Citation Audit

## Audit Method
Every section file was reviewed to confirm that FACT and DATA-BASED INFERENCE (DBI) claims carry an identifiable source (either inline or via reference to `18_RESEARCH/source-library.md`), and that CONSULTING ASSUMPTION (CA), SCENARIO ASSUMPTION (SA), and STRATEGIC JUDGMENT claims are explicitly labeled as such rather than presented as sourced fact.

## Audit Result
- No instance identified where a specific statistic is presented without a source label or citation reference.
- All pricing figures reference either a specific NPPA notification or a clearly labeled consumer-price-aggregator source (with the distinction between regulatory ceiling price and observed retail price made explicit where both are discussed).
- All Novo Nordisk-India-specific quantitative figures (share, revenue, margin) are explicitly labeled as consulting assumptions in `00_PROJECT_CHARTER/assumptions-register.md` and are not presented as disclosed company data anywhere in the report.

## Recommended Ongoing Practice
Given that NPPA/DPCO ceiling prices are revised annually (WPI-indexed) and ICMR-INDIAB/IDF prevalence figures are updated periodically, this citation set should be refreshed at least annually if the report is used beyond its original case-study purpose.
