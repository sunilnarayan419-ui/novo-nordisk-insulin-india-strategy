# Source Validation

## Source Quality Tiering
| Tier | Description | Examples Used |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 (highest) | Peer-reviewed journal / primary government notification | ICMR-INDIAB (Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology), NPPA price notifications, PIB press releases |
| Tier 2 | Reputable industry/market-research reports | IQVIA Institute, Grand View Research, Mordor Intelligence |
| Tier 3 | Reputable secondary aggregators / consumer-facing guides | IBEF, PolicyBazaar, Medstuffs, Medicover — used mainly for illustrative pricing ranges and program-scale figures, cross-checked against Tier 1/2 where possible |

## Validation Approach
Where a claim could be sourced at Tier 1, Tier 1 was used and cited as the primary reference. Tier 3 sources were used only for illustrative/consumer-facing figures (e.g., observed retail price ranges) where no Tier 1/2 equivalent was publicly available, and are explicitly flagged as such in the relevant section (e.g., `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/pricing-comparison.md`).

## Known Limitation
This engagement did not have access to paid/subscription industry databases (e.g., full IQVIA MIDAS data, paid market-research reports beyond publicly available summaries) — all sourcing is from freely accessible public information, consistent with the engagement's evidence-standard boundary (`00_PROJECT_CHARTER/problem-statement.md`).
