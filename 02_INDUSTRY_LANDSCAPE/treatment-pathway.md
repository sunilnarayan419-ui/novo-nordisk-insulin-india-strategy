# Clinical Treatment Pathway (Type 2 Diabetes → Insulin)

## Standard Escalation Pathway (FACT — consistent with ADA/EASD and Indian RSSDI-ESI clinical practice guidance)
1. Lifestyle modification (diet, physical activity)
2. Metformin monotherapy
3. Dual/triple oral or injectable non-insulin therapy (sulfonylureas, DPP-4 inhibitors, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists where accessible/affordable)
4. Basal insulin initiation (added to existing oral therapy) — typically triggered by HbA1c persistently above target despite optimized oral therapy, or by acute decompensation
5. Basal-plus / premix regimens
6. Basal-bolus (multiple daily injection) intensive therapy — for patients with advanced beta-cell failure

## Type 1 Diabetes Pathway (FACT)
Insulin-dependent from diagnosis; standard of care is basal-bolus multiple daily injection or insulin pump therapy, with continuous or intermittent glucose monitoring where affordable — device and monitoring cost, not insulin cost alone, is often the binding affordability constraint for this population in India.

## India-Specific Deviations from Guideline Pathway (DBI)
- **Premix-first pattern**: due to cost and regimen-simplicity pressure, many Indian patients move to human premix insulin at Stage 4/5 rather than following a guideline-preferred basal-first sequence — a pragmatic adaptation, with some clinical trade-off in flexibility and hypoglycemia risk.
- **GLP-1 emergence**: growing GLP-1 receptor agonist availability (including generics/biosimilar-adjacent entrants in India) is beginning to delay or reduce insulin initiation in higher-income Type 2 segments — a trend explicitly built into this report's financial model as a moderating factor on long-term insulin volume growth (see `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/`).

## Strategic Implication
Novo Nordisk's product and market-access strategy should be built around the actual India-specific pathway (premix-heavy, cost-constrained, GLP-1-influenced at the margin) rather than a Western guideline-idealized pathway — this shapes portfolio prioritization in `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/`.
