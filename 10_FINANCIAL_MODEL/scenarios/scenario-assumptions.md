# Scenario Assumptions (Narrative)

## Base Case
- Insulin-treated population CAGR: 7% (`market-growth-scenarios.md` consistency)
- Gradual analogue mix and channel-value growth, modest NN share gains across all channels (+1 to +5 points over 5 years depending on channel)
- Slight erosion of premium-channel margin (–2 points) reflecting anticipated competitive/price-control pressure, broadly flat elsewhere

## Upside Case
- Insulin-treated population CAGR: 9.5%
- Stronger NN share gains across all channels (+2 to +9 points), reflecting successful execution of the segmented/ecosystem strategy
- Margin holds or improves modestly in most channels, reflecting operating leverage and effective, well-targeted (not blanket) access-program spend

## Downside Case
- Insulin-treated population CAGR: 4.5%
- NN share flat-to-declining in premium and institutional channels (competitive/price-control pressure)
- Material margin compression across all channels (–2 to –8 points), reflecting a scenario where insulin glargine-class NLEM-style price control expands to additional analogue SKUs and biosimilar competition intensifies faster than anticipated

## Reading the Scenarios Together
The three scenarios are not simply "optimistic/pessimistic" multipliers on a single number — they reflect genuinely different **strategic execution and policy-environment states**, consistent with the qualitative scenario narrative in `../../03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/market-growth-scenarios.md`. The Upside Case specifically assumes the recommended strategy (`../../11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/recommended-strategy.md`) is executed well; the Downside Case is not simply "strategy fails" but specifically models an adverse *external* policy/competitive shock materializing regardless of execution quality — both are useful for stress-testing the recommendation's resilience.
