# Option Comparison Summary

| Dimension | A: Premium | B: Access | C: Segmented | D: Ecosystem |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core logic | Defend margin, serve the willing-to-pay | Chase volume via price | Match offer to segment economics | Build a durable, integrated moat |
| Biggest strength | Margin protection, low complexity | Access/patient impact | Balances access and margin genuinely | Highest sustainability and defensibility |
| Biggest weakness | Cedes majority market, access-mandate conflict | Financially fragile vs. low-cost domestic competition | Requires pricing/channel discipline to execute well | Highest execution complexity and investment need |
| Time to value | Fast | Fast (but fragile) | Moderate | Slow (multi-year build) |
| Risk profile | Reputational/regulatory risk | Financial/competitive risk | Execution-discipline risk | Capability/investment risk |

## Why Not a Pure A or B Choice
Both A and B fail the problem statement's explicit dual mandate — "expand access... while maintaining commercial viability" — in opposite directions: A protects viability at the cost of access; B pursues access at the cost of viability. Only C and D address both halves of the mandate simultaneously, which is why the recommendation is built from C, evolving to D.

See `recommended-strategy.md` for the final synthesized recommendation.
