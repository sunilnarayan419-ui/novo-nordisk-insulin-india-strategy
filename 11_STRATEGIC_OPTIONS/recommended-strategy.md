# Recommended Strategy

## The Recommendation, Stated Directly
**Novo Nordisk should adopt Option C (Segmented Portfolio + Differentiated Access Strategy) as the immediate operating model, with an explicit, funded roadmap to evolve into Option D (Ecosystem Strategy) over the 5-year horizon.** This is not a hedge between two options — it is a sequencing decision: build the segmented pricing/portfolio foundation first (feasible within 12–18 months), then layer in ecosystem elements (patient support, digital, provider training, institutional partnerships) as execution capability and initial results validate the investment (Years 2–5).

## What, For Whom, Where, How

- **What**: A three-tier portfolio (access/mainstream/premium, per `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/portfolio-strategy.md`) with channel-differentiated pricing (`06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/pricing-options.md`) and a phased ecosystem build (patient support, PMBJP distribution partnership, digital adherence tools, provider education).
- **For whom**: All insulin-treated segments, with the two highest-priority target populations being (a) mainstream private Tier 1/2 Type 2 patients at the initiation decision point, and (b) Type 1 diabetes patients nationally, regardless of income, via a dedicated patient-support program.
- **Where**: Geographic priority on Tier 1/2 cities in high-prevalence, mid-income states (Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, and similar — see `03_MARKET_ANALYSIS/geographic-opportunity.md`) in Year 1–2, expanding to Tier 3+/rural in Year 2–3 as cold-chain investment (`09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/cold-chain.md`) enables reliable distribution.
- **Pricing/access approach**: Channel-differentiated pricing, targeted patient-assistance for high-need segments, institutional volume contracts with private hospital networks, PMBJP partnership for human insulin distribution (`07_MARKET_ACCESS/market-access-strategy.md`).
- **Commercial model**: Segment-specific commercial motions — specialist detailing and premium-device marketing for the premium tier; general-physician education and simplified-initiation-protocol support for the mainstream tier; government/institutional relationship management and PMBJP partnership for the access tier.
- **Operational requirements**: Near-term investment in stock-out reduction and cold-chain partnerships (`09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/operational-opportunities.md`); medium-term investment in digital adherence tools and provider training infrastructure.

## Investment and Expected Return
See `12_IMPLEMENTATION/investment-requirements.md` for the phased investment plan and `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/README.md` for the associated financial projections: **Base Case Year-5 revenue of ~₹2,083 crore (~US$247M) at ~63% blended gross margin**, with the Upside Case (successful ecosystem execution) reaching **~₹2,806 crore (~US$332M)**.

## Key Risks
See `13_RISK_ANALYSIS/` for the full risk register; the most material risks are (1) further NLEM/price-control expansion compressing analogue margin faster than modeled, and (2) execution complexity of the segmented-pricing architecture creating channel leakage/arbitrage if not tightly governed.

## Why This Beats the Alternatives
This recommendation is the only one of the four (or their unweighted combination) that directly answers both halves of the mandate — expanding access and protecting long-term economics — with a financially modeled, phased, risk-aware path, rather than asserting the two goals are compatible without showing the mechanism (see `06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/access-vs-margin-tradeoff.md` for that mechanism explained in full).
