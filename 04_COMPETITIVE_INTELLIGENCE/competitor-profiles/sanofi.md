# Competitor Profile: Sanofi

## Overview
Long-standing glargine (Lantus/Toujeo) franchise; globally facing biosimilar erosion of glargine revenue, a dynamic already visible in India following NLEM addition of insulin glargine; India strategy likely defensive (protect installed base) rather than expansionary.

## Presence in India (FACT/DBI)
Active in the Indian insulin market across at least one of human insulin, analogue insulin, or biosimilar insulin categories (see `../competitor-landscape.md` for market-structure context). Country-level India revenue/share for this competitor's insulin business is not separately disclosed in public filings; positioning here is qualitative and directional.
