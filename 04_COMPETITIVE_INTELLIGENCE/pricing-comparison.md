# Pricing Comparison (Illustrative, India)

*All prices are illustrative retail/ceiling figures compiled from public NPPA notifications and public consumer price-comparison sources; actual brand-level prices vary and should be re-verified against the current NPPA/DPCO schedule before commercial use (see `18_RESEARCH/source-library.md`).*

| Product Category | Approx. Price Range (₹) | Price-Control Status |
|---|---|---|
| Human insulin (soluble/NPH/premix), 40 IU/ml vial | ~₹80–150/vial (NPPA ceiling ≈ ₹15.09/ml × ~10ml vial ≈ ₹150) | NLEM/DPCO price-controlled |
| Human insulin premix, branded, larger pack | ~₹150–400/vial | Price-controlled (formulation-level) |
| Analogue insulin (glargine), vial | ~₹450–900/vial (ceiling ≈ ₹217.74–244/ml under NLEM 2022) | Price-controlled since 2022 addition to NLEM |
| Analogue insulin (glargine), disposable pen | ~₹600–1,200/pen | Price-controlled (separate ceiling for pens) |
| Rapid-acting analogue (aspart/lispro), pen | ~₹700–1,500/pen | Largely outside strict NLEM control for non-glargine analogues (varies by SKU) |
| Premium long-acting analogue (e.g., degludec-class), pen | Reported consumer prices up to ~₹7,000+/pen | Not NLEM price-controlled |
| Biosimilar analogue (glargine/aspart), vial or pen | Meaningfully below originator price, often 30–60% lower | Competitive/market pricing |

## Key Takeaway
The price differential between the cheapest price-controlled human insulin (~₹15/ml) and the most expensive uncontrolled premium analogue pen (reportedly >₹7,000 for a pen) can approach **90x on a like-for-like hormone basis** (consistent with independent consumer price-guide estimates) — this dispersion is the single most important fact shaping India insulin strategy: it means "the insulin market" is really several structurally different markets operating side by side.

## Strategic Implication
Novo Nordisk's pricing architecture must explicitly manage this dispersion — offering price points across the full range rather than concentrating only at the premium end, which is the core logic of the segmented pricing recommendation (`06_PRICING_AFFORDABILITY/`).
