# Competitor Response Scenarios

## If Novo Nordisk Pursues a Segmented/Access Strategy (Option C/D)
- **Biocon Biologics** likely responds by further deepening government-tender penetration and possibly accelerating biosimilar aspart/glargine capacity, pressuring Novo Nordisk's institutional-channel volume further — but this is a channel Novo Nordisk was not fully winning regardless.
- **Sanofi/Lilly** likely respond selectively, given both have global strategic attention increasingly on GLP-1/obesity rather than defending insulin share in a price-controlled market — this reduces the likelihood of an aggressive multinational price war in India specifically.
- **Domestic human-insulin players** face limited incremental threat either way, as Novo Nordisk's access strategy targets analogue affordability more than displacing human-insulin government-tender volume.

## If Novo Nordisk Pursues a Premium-Only Strategy (Option A)
- Biosimilar and domestic competitors gain uncontested share in the price-sensitive majority of the market, and Novo Nordisk's addressable patient base (and thus long-term volume/relationship equity with physicians) shrinks — a "winning the premium battle, losing the market war" outcome.

## If Novo Nordisk Pursues an Aggressive Price-Led Access Strategy (Option B, undifferentiated)
- Risk of a margin race-to-the-bottom against domestic manufacturers who have a structurally lower cost base — Novo Nordisk is unlikely to win a pure price war against India-based biosimilar manufacturing, making this option financially fragile (see `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/option-B-access-strategy.md`).

## Regulatory Response Risk (Cross-Cutting)
Any option that is publicly framed as "raising prices" or "reducing access" carries reputational and regulatory risk (further NLEM expansion) — conversely, a credibly access-oriented strategy (Options C/D) provides some insulation against further adverse price-control action by demonstrating good-faith access investment, a pattern seen in how governments have responded to companies with visible access programs in other therapeutic areas.
