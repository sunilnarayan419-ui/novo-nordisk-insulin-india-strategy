# Pricing Landscape

## Regulatory Pricing Framework (FACT)
- The **Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013 (DPCO)**, administered by the **National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA)**, sets ceiling prices for "scheduled formulations" listed in the **National List of Essential Medicines (NLEM)**.
- Human insulin (soluble, NPH, premix 30:70, all at 40 IU/ml) has long been NLEM-scheduled: NPPA fixed a ceiling price of **₹15.09/ml** for these formulations (per public NPPA notification data).
- **Insulin glargine was newly added to NLEM in 2022** — a significant policy shift extending price control to a modern analogue for the first time. NPPA fixed ceiling prices separately for vials/cartridges (~₹217.74/ml, later WPI-adjusted to ~₹244.13/ml effective April 2023) and for disposable pens, following representations from Sanofi India and industry bodies (OPPI, USISPF) that vials and pens should be treated as distinct scheduled formulations.
- Non-NLEM-scheduled formulations (most rapid-acting analogues, premium long-acting analogues, premix analogues) are **not directly price-controlled**, though DPCO imposes an annual MRP increase cap even on non-scheduled formulations.

## Observed Retail Price Range (DBI, from public consumer price-comparison sources)
Consumer-facing price guides report insulin retail prices in India ranging from **as low as ~₹88 for a government-regulated human insulin vial to over ₹7,700 for a premium long-acting analogue pen** — a reported ~90x spread for comparable therapeutic purpose.

## Interpretation
India runs a **dual pricing regime**: a tightly regulated, low-margin human-insulin/glargine-analogue floor, and a largely unregulated premium-analogue ceiling. This is a structural feature companies must plan around, not a temporary anomaly — and the direction of regulatory travel (glargine's 2022 NLEM addition) suggests further analogue categories could be added to price control within the report's 5-year horizon.

## Strategic Implication
Pricing strategy must be built scenario-tested against **further NLEM expansion**, not just current price levels (see `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/sensitivity-analysis/` for the price-control sensitivity case).
