# Affordability Analysis

## Framing: Cost Relative to Income
Public reporting indicates a typical Indian family managing diabetes (particularly Type 1 in a child, or intensive Type 2 regimens) can spend **₹6,000–10,000/month** out of pocket on diabetes care including insulin, syringes/pen needles, and glucose monitoring supplies (consistent with patient-facing cost-guide reporting) — a substantial burden relative to median Indian household income, and a catastrophic burden for lower-income households.

## Affordability by Segment (CA, illustrative — see `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/` for the formal model)
| Segment | Illustrative Monthly Insulin Cost (₹) | Illustrative Share of Household Income |
|---|---|---|
| Premium private/insured | 2,000–5,000+ | <5% (manageable, often insurance-buffered for the medical-treatment component even if outpatient drugs are not covered) |
| Mainstream private (Tier 1/2) | 800–2,500 | 5–15% |
| Government/institutional-supported | 100–500 (subsidized/tender-priced or free at point of care where available) | <2% where access exists, but access itself is inconsistent |
| Rural/out-of-pocket, no subsidy | 300–1,200 (even at "cheap" human-insulin pricing, cost is a larger share of a smaller income) | 10–25%+, frequently catastrophic |

## Consequence of Poor Affordability (FACT/DBI)
Cost-driven rationing (skipping doses, reducing dose below prescription, delaying refills) is a well-documented driver of **diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)** in insulin-dependent patients and of long-term complications in undertreated Type 2 patients — meaning affordability gaps translate directly into excess morbidity, not just unrealized commercial demand.

## Strategic Implication
Affordability interventions should be prioritized where the **income share burden is highest and least buffered** — rural/out-of-pocket and lower-income Type 1 households — which is the target population for the patient-assistance program component of the recommended strategy (see `08_HEALTH_ECONOMICS/` and `11_STRATEGIC_OPTIONS/option-D-ecosystem-strategy.md`).
