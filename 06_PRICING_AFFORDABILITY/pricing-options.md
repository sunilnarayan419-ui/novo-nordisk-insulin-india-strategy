# Pricing Options

## Option 1 — Uniform National Pricing (status-quo-like baseline)
Single price per SKU nationally. Simple to administer, but leaves both ends of the market underserved: too expensive for price-sensitive channels, potentially leaving margin on the table in the premium channel if set to a blended middle price.

## Option 2 — Channel-Differentiated Pricing
Different pricing for institutional/government tenders vs. private retail vs. patient-support-program-enrolled patients, within regulatory bounds. Requires strong internal controls to prevent channel leakage/arbitrage, but directly addresses the affordability dispersion documented in `pricing-landscape.md`.

## Option 3 — Tiered Pack-Size / Format Strategy
Offer smaller, lower-absolute-cost pack sizes (e.g., smaller-volume vials or shorter-duration cartridge options) to reduce the upfront cash outlay for lower-income patients, even if per-unit price is similar — addresses a liquidity/cash-flow barrier distinct from headline price.

## Option 4 — Patient Assistance / Co-Pay Support Programs
Manufacturer-funded or NGO-partnered co-pay assistance for defined patient segments (e.g., Type 1 diabetes, documented low-income Type 2 patients), similar in spirit to global manufacturer patient-assistance programs (e.g., Eli Lilly's global Insulin Value Program capping patient out-of-pocket cost, though implemented for the US market specifically).

## Option 5 — Institutional Value-Based / Volume Contracts
Multi-year volume-committed pricing with large hospital networks or state governments in exchange for guaranteed volume and preferred-formulary status — trades headline unit price for volume certainty and demand-planning efficiency (supply-chain benefit, see `09_SUPPLY_CHAIN/inventory-economics.md`).

## Recommended Combination (STRATEGIC JUDGMENT)
A combination of **Option 2 (channel-differentiated pricing) + Option 4 (targeted patient assistance) + Option 5 (institutional volume contracts)**, layered onto the segmented portfolio architecture in `05_PRODUCT_AND_PORTFOLIO/portfolio-strategy.md`, best balances access expansion against margin preservation — full economics modeled in `10_FINANCIAL_MODEL/`.
