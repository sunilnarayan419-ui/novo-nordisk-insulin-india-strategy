# Portfolio Summary

**Project**: Strategic Assessment of Insulin Access, Affordability, and Portfolio Competitiveness in India (Novo Nordisk case study)

**Type**: Independent life-sciences/biotech commercial strategy case study, built entirely from publicly available information.

**Scope**: 20-section strategy report covering market sizing, patient segmentation, competitive intelligence, product/portfolio strategy, pricing and affordability analysis, market access, health economics, supply chain/cold-chain diagnostics, a quantitative scenario-based financial model (Python, with sensitivity analysis on 8 variables), four strategic options with a weighted scorecard, an implementation roadmap, and a full risk register.

**Skills demonstrated**: Market sizing/TAM-SAM-SOM construction from public epidemiological data; competitive intelligence synthesis; health-economics reasoning (total cost of care framework); pricing/access strategy design under regulatory price control; supply-chain/cold-chain diagnostic analysis for a temperature-sensitive biologic; quantitative financial modeling in Python (scenario and sensitivity analysis); strategic option evaluation via a weighted decision framework; and structured, evidence-labeled consulting writing (FACT / DATA-BASED INFERENCE / CONSULTING ASSUMPTION / SCENARIO ASSUMPTION / STRATEGIC JUDGMENT).

**Deliverable format**: Structured markdown-based repository (20 sections + appendices), original SVG diagrams (insulin mechanism of action, manufacturing pathway, patient journey, distribution flow, cold-chain risk map), and a reproducible Python quantitative model with CSV outputs.
