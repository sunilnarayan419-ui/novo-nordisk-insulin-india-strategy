# LinkedIn Project Description

**Independent Case Study: Novo Nordisk India Insulin Strategy**

I built a full strategy-consulting case study addressing a real, publicly discussed challenge: how should a global insulin leader expand access and affordability in India's diabetes market — one of the largest and most price-diverse insulin markets in the world — without eroding the commercial sustainability of its business?

Using only public data (ICMR-INDIAB epidemiological research, NPPA/DPCO pricing notifications, government program documentation, and industry market-research summaries), I built:
- A triangulated market sizing model (TAM/SAM/SOM) for India's insulin-treated population
- A competitive intelligence view of the global insulin oligopoly's very different, fragmented dynamics inside India
- A segmented pricing and portfolio strategy addressing a >15x price gap between price-controlled human insulin and premium analogues
- A Python-based quantitative model with three scenarios and sensitivity analysis on 8 variables
- Four evaluated strategic options, a weighted scorecard, and a phased implementation roadmap
- Original diagrams explaining insulin's biology, manufacturing pathway, and India's cold-chain distribution challenges

This was a chance to combine scientific/biotech understanding with commercial strategy, data-driven modeling, and clear executive communication — exactly the kind of work at the intersection of life sciences and business I'm building toward. Full write-up available on request.
