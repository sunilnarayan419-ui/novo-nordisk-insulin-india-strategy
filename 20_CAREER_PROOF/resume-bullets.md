# Resume Bullets

- Built an end-to-end market-entry/growth strategy case study for insulin access in India, sizing a ~₹5,000–12,000 crore addressable market from public epidemiological data (ICMR-INDIAB) and triangulated industry sources.
- Designed a segmented pricing and portfolio architecture reconciling patient-access expansion with margin protection, addressing a >15x regulatory price differential between human insulin and analogue insulin under India's NPPA/DPCO framework.
- Built a Python-based quantitative scenario model (Base/Upside/Downside) with 8-variable sensitivity analysis, projecting 5-year revenue and margin outcomes and identifying market share and pricing as the two highest-leverage strategic variables.
- Evaluated four distinct strategic options against a 10-criterion weighted scorecard, producing a phased strategic recommendation (segmented portfolio strategy evolving into an integrated ecosystem model) with an implementation roadmap and governance structure.
- Conducted a supply-chain/cold-chain diagnostic for a temperature-sensitive biologic across India's fragmented last-mile distribution network, identifying operational levers with the highest access-impact-to-cost ratio.
- Produced original scientific and operational diagrams (mechanism of action, manufacturing pathway, distribution flow, cold-chain risk map) to support technical and executive communication of complex biotech supply-chain and biology concepts.
