# Value Proposition (by Audience)

## To Government / Payers
"Appropriately managed insulin therapy — matched to patient risk level — reduces the downstream cost of diabetes to the health system. We will partner on outpatient access pilots and provide the evidence base to support fiscally sound chronic-disease financing design, not just push volume."

## To Institutions / Hospital Networks
"A segmented portfolio lets your formulary serve both cost-sensitive and complex patients appropriately, backed by device and clinical-education support that reduces dosing errors and readmissions."

## To Physicians
"Access to a full spectrum of insulin options — from affordable human insulin to advanced analogues — plus simplified initiation protocols and patient-education materials, to reduce clinical inertia in insulin initiation and support real-world adherence."

## To Patients / Households
"Options across price points, delivery formats, and support programs so that starting and staying on insulin doesn't mean choosing between control and affordability."

## To Insurers / TPAs
"Outpatient insulin and monitoring support can reduce your downstream claims cost from diabetes complications — we will partner on pilot programs with shared cost-avoidance measurement."

## Common Thread
Every version of the value proposition foregrounds **total cost of care and access**, not clinical superiority alone — consistent with the health-economics analysis in this section and the market reality that biosimilar/domestic competitors can credibly contest a clinical-superiority-only narrative.
