# Clinical Outcome → Economic Value Linkage

## Outcome Chain
Insulin therapy quality → glycemic control (HbA1c) and hypoglycemia frequency → complication incidence (retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy, cardiovascular disease, DKA) → healthcare utilization and cost → productivity and quality-of-life impact.

## Key Linkages (FACT, general clinical-outcomes literature, applied directionally to India)
- Sustained HbA1c reduction is associated with materially reduced microvascular complication risk (well-established from long-term diabetes outcome trials internationally); the relationship holds regardless of which specific insulin type achieves the HbA1c reduction, reinforcing that **any effective, sustained therapy** — human or analogue — captures most of this value, provided it is actually used consistently.
- Reduced hypoglycemia frequency (where analogues show a clearer relative advantage) is linked to fewer emergency visits/hospitalizations and, in elderly patients, reduced fall/fracture risk and preserved cognitive function over time — a more analogue-specific value channel than HbA1c control alone.

## Quantification Challenge (Evidentiary Caveat)
Precise India-specific quantification of "how many complications are avoided per additional analogue-year of therapy" is not available from public sources at the granularity this report would need for a formal cost-effectiveness ratio (e.g., cost per QALY gained) — the report therefore presents this linkage qualitatively and directionally rather than asserting a specific ICER (incremental cost-effectiveness ratio) for India, to avoid overstating precision the public evidence base does not support.

## Strategic Implication
Any future formal health-economic submission to Indian payers/regulators (e.g., supporting inclusion in a future government program) should commission or partner on an India-specific real-world evidence study, given the current gap in publicly available India-specific cost-effectiveness data — this is flagged as a medium-term strategic investment opportunity (see `12_IMPLEMENTATION/capability-requirements.md`).
