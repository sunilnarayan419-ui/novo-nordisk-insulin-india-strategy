# Health Economic Assumptions Register

| ID | Assumption | Type | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| HE1 | A single acute hypoglycemia-related hospitalization costs several multiples of one year's insulin therapy cost, even at premium analogue pricing | CA | General international health-economics literature pattern, applied directionally |
| HE2 | Complication-avoidance value from improved glycemic control accrues predominantly over a 5+ year horizon | DBI | Consistent with long-term diabetes-outcomes trial literature internationally |
| HE3 | Analogue insulin's incremental value over human insulin is concentrated in hypoglycemia-risk subgroups, not uniform across all Type 2 patients | STRATEGIC JUDGMENT / DBI | Synthesis of comparative-effectiveness literature |
| HE4 | No India-specific, publicly available cost-per-QALY figure exists for analogue vs. human insulin at the granularity needed for formal cost-effectiveness claims | FACT (evidentiary gap) | Absence of identified public India-specific study in research conducted for this report |
| HE5 | AB PM-JAY's hospitalization-only design creates a structural mismatch between where diabetes costs are paid (acute complications) and where prevention investment would be most cost-effective (routine outpatient care) | DBI | Derived from PM-JAY program design documentation |

All health-economic figures in this report should be treated as **directional/illustrative** for strategy-setting purposes, not as validated inputs for a regulatory health-technology-assessment submission.
