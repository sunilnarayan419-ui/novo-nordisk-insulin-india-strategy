# Treatment Cost Model (Per-Patient, Illustrative)

## Annual Cost Build by Regimen (CA, illustrative INR)

| Regimen | Insulin cost/year | Consumables/year | Routine visits/year | **Total/year** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human premix, twice daily (Type 2) | ~2,500–5,000 | ~1,500–2,500 | ~1,000–2,000 | **~5,000–9,500** |
| Basal analogue + oral combo (Type 2) | ~6,000–12,000 | ~1,500–2,500 | ~1,500–2,500 | **~9,000–17,000** |
| Basal-bolus analogue, intensive (Type 1 or advanced Type 2) | ~18,000–40,000 | ~4,000–8,000 (incl. more frequent monitoring) | ~2,500–5,000 | **~24,500–53,000** |

*Figures are triangulated from NPPA ceiling prices, public consumer insulin-cost guides, and general diabetes-care cost literature; treat as directional order-of-magnitude, not billing-grade estimates.*

## Cost of Complications (Comparative Benchmark, DBI)
A single DKA hospitalization or cardiovascular event is consistently reported in health-economics literature (across multiple health systems, adapted directionally for India) as costing several multiples of a full year of even intensive insulin therapy — meaning the "expensive" intensive regimen is frequently the *cheaper* long-run option once complication-avoidance is priced in.
